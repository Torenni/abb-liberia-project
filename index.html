The policy challenge

Public spending should be connected to measurable results.

Liberia’s public financial management system records what government authorizes and spends, but available reporting offers limited evidence linking expenditures to the activities, services, and outcomes they support. This paper proposes a practical, phased approach to strengthening that connection without replacing existing budget controls.

The proposed approach

A practical framework for Activity-Based Budgeting

The article proposes a dual-coded ABB framework: retain administrative and economic classifications for financial control while connecting allocations to programs, activities, institutions, outputs, indicators, targets, costs, funding sources, and locations.

01 · Preserve controls

Maintain existing expenditure classifications and legal accountability.

02 · Trace implementation

Link appropriations and expenditures to defined activities, services, and outputs.

03 · Learn and improve

Use verified financial and performance evidence to guide budget decisions and phased expansion.
